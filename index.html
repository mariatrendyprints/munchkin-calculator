Gear — one Headgear, one Armor, one Footgear, two hands (tick 2‑handed and the second hand is ignored), plus any number of no‑slot Misc items. Type the card's name if you like and set the bonus from the card. Misc items have an On/Off switch for cards that only work under a condition — flip it off and the bonus stays on the card but drops out of the total.

Allies are the Door cards that fight beside you — Royalty can keep more than one. Character is your character card's power when it applies this fight (Finn: +2 vs. Level 10+ monsters). Class bonus is what Wizard / Royalty / Hero / Musician adds this fight. Curses are subtracted. One‑shots are potions and snacks for this fight only.

The fight — add the monster, set its level and any modifier from enhancer cards or the card's own text; use + Monster for extra monsters. Every player card then shows whether they beat the combined monster strength. End battle clears the monsters and everyone's one‑shots.
